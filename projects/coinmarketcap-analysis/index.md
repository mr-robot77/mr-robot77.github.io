I pulled CoinMarketCap market data into Python and cleaned it: missing values, outliers, and one scale for the series. The download step can run again when new rows show up.

The charts cover price over time, market-cap distribution, and correlation between major coins, including Bitcoin and Ethereum. Those large coins often move together. Market cap is uneven across categories, and the prices show repeated swings rather than one trend.
